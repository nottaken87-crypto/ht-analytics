Privacy Policy — Hollow Terrors Analytics

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Hollow Terrors Analytics is a private tool operated by the owner of the TikTok account @hollowterrorschannel. It is used only by that account's owner and only for that account.

What the tool accesses

Through TikTok's official Display API, and only with the account owner's authorization, the tool reads: basic profile information for @hollowterrorschannel (user.info.basic) and the account's own video list including per-video view counts (video.list).

What the tool does not do

It does not access any other person's account or data. It does not collect data about viewers or commenters. It does not sell, share, rent, or disclose any data to third parties. It does not post or modify content.

Storage and retention

Retrieved view counts are stored in the channel owner's own analytics records for content planning. Authorization tokens are stored in secure credential storage and are never shared. The account owner can revoke the tool's access at any time in TikTok's settings; on revocation, stored tokens are deleted.
